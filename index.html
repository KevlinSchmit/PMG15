THE PROBLEM

Accidental
bycatch.

Recreational anglers can unintentionally hook protected animals because they cannot always see what is approaching their bait underwater.

Although people could release them immediately back to the ocean, hooks can injure the mouth, head, or digestive tract. Swallowed hooks can interfere with feeding and cause starvation and death.

NOAANOAA states that accidental capture or injury of sea turtles and marine mammals during fishing is a significant threat to their health and recovery.

THE ORIGINAL CONCEPT ↘BLUE CATCH / PRODUCT DEFINITION
PRODUCT DEFINITION

Blue Catch is an AI-powered smart fishing rod designed to help anglers avoid accidentally hooking protected marine animals. It uses a camera attached to fish-shaped bait to identify nearby fish and automatically retracts its hooks when a protected species is detected. A screen on the fishing rod displays the animal’s photo, an interactive 3D model, and fun facts. Our mission is to protect marine life while making fishing safer and more educational.

  1. 01

    Camera on fish-shaped bait with retractable hooks

    • Includes AI and when spotting a fish nearby, the AI will compare it to endangered species to determine whether that fish is one
    • If endangered then the hooks will retract into the bait to avoid injuring the fish immediately
    • Hooks will remain retracted until that fish had left
  2. 02

    Screen on the pole

    Shows the endangered species captured as an interactive 3D model, along with the photo camera just took with fun facts about it

  3. 03

    Floats equipped with lights that can change color

    • Will light up when hooks are retracted to notify the user
    • If at night will change color since the float lights up at night already
