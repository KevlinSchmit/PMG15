THE PROBLEM

Accidental
bycatch.

Recreational anglers can unintentionally hook protected animals because they cannot always see what is approaching their bait underwater.

Although people could release them immediately back to the ocean, hooks can injure the mouth, head, or digestive tract. Swallowed hooks can interfere with feeding and cause starvation and death.

NOAANOAA states that accidental capture or injury of sea turtles and marine mammals during fishing is a significant threat to their health and recovery.

LARGE POTENTIAL MARKET / U.S. / 2022

In the U.S. alone, 12.7 million people participated in saltwater fishing in 2022, making approximately 104 million saltwater fishing trips.

Exponential Drivers↗

01

Artificial intelligence (AI)

  • Rapid advances in computer vision are making real-time species recognition faster and more accurate based on physical features (ex. body shape, color, size)
  • Smaller and more powerful AI chips allow complex image recognition to run directly on compact devices with minimal delay
02

Miniaturized camera technology

  • Cameras are becoming smaller and lighter while maintaining high image quality
  • Tiny waterproof cameras can now be integrated into fishing equipment without affecting usability
03

Real-time 3D visualization

  • Improvements in 3D rendering technology make interactive visualizations more realistic
  • Advances in computing power allows detailed 3D models to run on everyday devices
  • Interactive visualization adds a new layer of engagement & educational content

Our Solution.

Smart fishing equipment
  1. 01

    Camera on fish-shaped bait with retractable hooks

    • Includes AI and when spotting a fish nearby, the AI will compare it to endangered species to determine whether that fish is one
    • If endangered then the hooks will retract into the bait to avoid injuring the fish immediately
    • Hooks will remain retracted until that fish had left
  2. 02

    Screen on the pole

    Shows the endangered species captured as an interactive 3D model, along with the photo camera just took with fun facts about it

  3. 03

    Floats equipped with lights that can change color

    • Will light up when hooks are retracted to notify the user
    • If at night will change color since the float lights up at night already

Value Proposition.

For anglers who want to avoid accidentally hooking protected marine animals and learn more about marine life, Blue Catch is an AI-powered smart fishing rod that identifies nearby fish, automatically retracts its hooks when protected species are detected, and displays the animal’s photo, interactive 3D model, and fun facts to help prevent injuries to marine life while making fishing safer and more educational.
