BLUE CATCH / GROUP 16ORIGINAL PAGE 20

Value Proposition.

For anglers who want to avoid accidentally hooking protected marine animals and learn more about marine life, Blue Catch is an AI-powered smart fishing rod that identifies nearby fish, automatically retracts its hooks when protected species are detected, and displays the animal’s photo, interactive 3D model, and fun facts to help prevent injuries to marine life while making fishing safer and more educational.

Our Solution.

One connected protection system
  1. 01

    Camera bait + retractable hooks

    Identifies nearby species and retracts automatically before a protected fish can be injured.

  2. 02

    Screen on the rod

    Shows the captured image, species name and essential facts in real time.

  3. 03

    Color-changing smart float

    A visible signal confirms protection mode is active—day or night.

SWOT Analysis
of BlueCatch

S

Strengths

  • Prevents harm before contact
  • AI recognition with low latency
  • Built-in 3D learning experience
W

Weaknesses

  • Variable underwater visibility
  • More hardware than a standard float
  • Accuracy depends on species data
O

Opportunities

  • Large recreational fishing market
  • Growing conservation awareness
  • Expandable to monitoring and aquaculture
T

Threats

  • Established camera and sensor brands
  • False alarms can reduce trust
  • Location-data privacy concerns
